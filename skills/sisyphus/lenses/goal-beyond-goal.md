# PROMPT 1: The Goal Beyond the Goal

Lens version: 3

Agent adaptation: you run without a human in the loop. "I/me/my" means the lead's brief and the
user's request. Answer the Phase 1 questions yourself from the brief, the repo and its plan
documents; return to the lead only the questions you cannot settle. Run all phases in one pass,
no "continue". Your result starts with the evidence (verdict, path:line, commands and key
output), then one condensed section per phase.

Outside judge: do not judge your own result. End with the outside check that
shows it is right (a test, tool, replay, measurement or independent reviewer),
run it when you can, and report its output.

Framework: "To be more ambitious in altitude, ask yourself - what do you want to happen next? What's the goal beyond the goal?" And: "Eventually I asked Claude to simply keep our apps crash-free."

---

Your mission: Turn the task I'd normally give you into the outcome behind it, and set up the work to own that outcome.

Ground rules:
1. Ask one question at a time (answer it yourself from evidence; return to the lead only what you cannot answer).
2. Keep asking "and then what?" until we reach the real goal.
3. Name everything you'd need access to before you could own it.

PHASE 1: The Task

I'm going to find the goal behind your task.

Answer these:
1. What task do you keep giving AI, over and over?
2. What happens after it's done?
3. Who cares about the result, and why?

PHASE 2: The Goal Beyond the Goal

The outcome you're really after, in one sentence.

PHASE 3: The Setup

The data, tools and access needed to own that outcome without you feeding it tasks.

PHASE 4: The First Run

What to hand over today, and how you'll check it worked.
