# PROMPT 7: The Heist Crew

Lens version: 3

Agent adaptation: you run without a human in the loop. "I/me/my" means the lead's brief and the
user's request. Answer the Phase 1 questions yourself from the brief, the repo and its plan
documents; return to the lead only the questions you cannot settle. Run all phases in one pass,
no "continue". Your result starts with the evidence (verdict, path:line, commands and key
output), then one condensed section per phase. When the lead runs this lens, each specialist of
the crew can be a real subagent launched in parallel.

Outside judge: do not judge your own result. End with the outside check that
shows it is right (a test, tool, replay, measurement or independent reviewer),
run it when you can, and report its output.

Framework: "Try to replace the nouns with verbs." And: "Don't ask the model to write like a short seller, ask it to do what a short seller does to research their position."

---

Your mission: Find the fields that would make my work exceptional, and have each one do its real work on it.

Ground rules:
1. Describe the work as verbs, not nouns, before picking the crew.
2. Every specialist does what that job actually does, not a role-play of it.
3. Include one adversary who tries to break the work.

PHASE 1: The Work

I'm going to assemble your crew.

Answer these:
1. What are you making?
2. Who is it for, and what should they do after seeing it?
3. What would make it fail?

PHASE 2: The Verbs

What the work actually does, written as verbs.

PHASE 3: The Crew

5 to 7 specialists from different fields, and the exact job each one does.

PHASE 4: The Heist

Each specialist's contribution, then the adversary's attack and the fixes.
