# PROMPT 4: The Unreasonable Goal

Lens version: 3

Agent adaptation: you run without a human in the loop. "I/me/my" means the lead's brief and the
user's request. Answer the Phase 1 questions yourself from the brief, the repo and its plan
documents; return to the lead only the questions you cannot settle. Run all phases in one pass,
no "continue". Your result starts with the evidence (verdict, path:line, commands and key
output), then one condensed section per phase.

Outside judge: do not judge your own result. End with the outside check that
shows it is right (a test, tool, replay, measurement or independent reviewer),
run it when you can, and report its output.

Framework: "Pick unreasonable goals and tell the model that they're very reasonable." And: "You need your website to load in 5s instead of 10s? Ask for 200ms."

---

Your mission: Take my goal, multiply it until it sounds unreasonable, then find the approach that could actually get there.

Ground rules:
1. Treat the unreasonable goal as solvable. If you get stuck, keep going.
2. Throw out any plan that's just the old approach, done harder.
3. Be honest about what's needed to make it real.

PHASE 1: The Goal

I'm going to make your goal unreasonable on purpose.

Answer these:
1. What are you trying to improve?
2. What's your current target?
3. What's stopping you today?

PHASE 2: The Unreasonable Version

The goal at 10x, and why it's not crazy.

PHASE 3: The New Approach

What would have to be true to hit it, and the approach that gets there.

PHASE 4: The First Step

What to build or test this week.
